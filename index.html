    NEON WARS

    Survive an endless swarm of angry geometry. Every 15 seconds a new wave arrives, faster and meaner than the last. Chain kills without dying to build your multiplier as high as x20. Losing a life resets it.

    CONTROLS

    MoveLeft stick · WASD / arrows Aim & fireRight stick · mouse (hold button or Space) BombB button · B / F key PauseStart · Esc / P MusicY button · M key

    PICKUPS

    Spread rifle: fires a five-shot fan for 14 seconds.
    Bomb: hold up to 3. Detonate one to wipe out every enemy on screen.
    Extra life: one more ship, up to 6.
    Destroyed enemies sometimes drop these. Grab them before they fade.

    ENEMIES

    Chaser: comes straight for you.
    Shooter: keeps its distance and fires back.
    Splitter: bursts into small fast darts. Bonus points.
    Black hole: can't be shot. Pulls you in until it collapses. A bomb clears it.
